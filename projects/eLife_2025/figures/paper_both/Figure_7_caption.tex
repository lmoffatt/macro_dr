\caption{\textbf{What a macroscopic recording can be asked for, and which method can deliver it.} The design plane, channel number $N_{\mathrm{ch}}$ against dimensionless instrumental noise, at one acquisition interval ($\Delta\!\cdot\!k_{\mathrm{off}}=0.1$) and no other. Every cell is filled with which parameters survive there, counting one as recovered when its distortion-corrected standard error is inside a factor of two, and the fills give five regions, listed in the key in their vertical order on the panel so that the key reads as a section through the map: near-white, where nothing is measurable; amber, where the rates alone survive and classical least squares (\texttt{ILSE}) is calibrated there, so the cheaper method is the one to use; pale green, where the rates alone survive but least squares reports an interval that is too narrow; dark green, where the unitary conductance $i$ is recovered as well, with $k_{\mathrm{off}}$ and usually with $k_{\mathrm{on}}$ and $N_{\mathrm{ch}}$; and, hatched over that colour rather than replacing it, the corner in which the recursive likelihood's own interval (\texttt{IR}) is not calibrated, where the parameters are still recovered and it is the report that has to be corrected. Two classes of line cross the plane and are drawn differently because they answer different questions: a white line cased in dark marks where a method's reported error bar becomes calibrated (information distortion $=1.15$, a $7\%$ error on the reported standard deviation), one per method, and a thin black line marks where a parameter stops being measurable, one per parameter. Two of the four black lines carry the partition, those for $i$ and $k_{\mathrm{off}}$; the other two subdivide the dark green region without partitioning it. Each line is a power law fitted through its own measured crossings, drawn as open dots, solid over the range of channel numbers in which those crossings were found and dashed outside it, with slopes $+1.11$ for $i$, $+1.47$ for $N_{\mathrm{ch}}$, $+2.09$ for $k_{\mathrm{off}}$, $+2.20$ for $k_{\mathrm{on}}$, $+1.03$ for the calibration of least squares and $-0.50$ for that of the likelihood, the one boundary with a negative slope because instrumental noise makes the emission more Gaussian and so repairs the closure. The partition is measured rather than imposed: only six subsets of the four parameters occur anywhere in the $80$ cells and they nest at this resolution with no counterexample, and classifying every cell twice, once from its own measurements and once from the fitted lines, $78$ of the $80$ agree, the two failures falling outside the support of the fit that misplaces them. Least squares holds the unitary current fixed and cannot separate the channel count from it, so over the $63$ cells that recover the conductance it has nothing to report about the amplitude at all, which is a property of the method and not a finding about its error bar; where the two do compete, over the $32$ cells in which both were run, its corrected standard error on the rates is no wider, at a median ratio of $1.04$ on $k_{\mathrm{off}}$ and $1.01$ on $k_{\mathrm{on}}$. Filled dots are the $80$ simulated cells, $5$ to $10^4$ channels and $0.005$ to $10^6$ in dimensionless noise; past the last simulated column the fills are veiled and the veil is labelled, so the $56\%$ of the width that carries no cell is visible as such. The noise axis is dimensionless by construction, $\widetilde{S} = \sigma^2/(B\,\tau\,i^2)$ with $\sigma$ the RMS current noise in a bandwidth $B$, $\tau = 1/k_{\mathrm{off}}$ the relaxation time and $i$ the unitary current, so that a whole-cell rig at $1$ pA RMS in a $1$ kHz band recording $1$ pA channels with a $10$ ms relaxation sits at $\widetilde{S} = 0.1$; no second axis in picoamps is drawn, because the conversion goes as $i^2$ and an axis placed at an assumed $1$ pA would be two decades wrong for a $10$ pA channel. Three recording configurations are placed on the same plane as heavy outlines with reversed tabs, an excised outside-out patch at $10$ to $300$ channels, whole cell at $20$ to $10^4$, and an oocyte under two-electrode voltage clamp (TEVC) at $10^5$ to $5\times10^7$: their channel ranges are derived from the smallest and largest current each configuration can hold, while their vertical extent is read from published traces and is indicative. The map is a concept map and not a phase diagram. Its boundaries are level sets of continuous diagnostics, so the criterion moves them: loosening the measurability criterion from a factor of two to a factor of ten lifts the four parameter boundaries by a median factor of $9.1$ to $21.2$ in noise, and loosening the calibration criterion from $1.15$ to $1.30$ lowers the least-squares boundary by a factor of $0.47$ to $0.50$. The layout survives that and the positions do not, so no cell should be read to better than about a decade in noise. All of it is the two-state scheme at a single open probability and a single concentration-jump protocol.}
