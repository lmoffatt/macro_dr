**The thread of the Introduction,** chosen over four alternatives in a controlled comparison: a
least-squares fit is judged from its residual, whose amplitude and whose temporal correlation are both
readable against knowledge from outside the fit; modelling the correlation is what takes both readings

1888 words against the predecessor's 2095, which is kept at

## 2. What happened today, in the order it matters

**Three errors were found and fixed.** The coverage numbers no longer reproduced: re-running the
committed script gives IR 2116/2268 where the manuscript had 1977/2100, because the freeze
directories grew. Appendix 1's `av=0` equation carried the full-window propagator where half a window
is called for, which historically produced a distortion-induced-bias spike in the recursive
instantaneous member. And the per-channel convention for the occupancy moments was used throughout
Theory and declared nowhere, which is worse than a contradiction because nothing looks wrong.

**Four recipes were added, all transplanted from material that already existed in `theory/`.** The
boundary-conditioned interval moments Γ̄ and V̄, as the two augmented generators, which block of each
exponential to read, and the endpoint-average limit for the pre-pulse where the transition
probability is exactly zero. The magnitude and anisotropy summaries with their formulas. The
near-singular anchor rule with its tolerance. And the numerical safeguard, whose damping has to be
infinitely differentiable *because this paper differentiates the log-likelihood*, with its two
constants.

**The captions were audited against seven published eLife papers** (medians 150 to 260 words, maximum
447). Ours were within norm on length and carried results that belonged in the text. Moving them out
revealed that **Figure 5 had a caption and no prose**: its two measured results lived only in its
legend, which is why the legend ran to 412 words with two findings in bold. It now has a subsection.

**Two replication audits were run**, six agents each, a PhD student per stage who may read only the
nine section files. Run 1: 94 blockers, stopped at the word "uniformization", whose natural reading
gives a plateau variance inflated by a factor of the channel count with nothing in the paper to catch
it. Run 2, after the fixes: 110 blockers, but the hard category fell and the whole increase is in
the softest one. The four places where four of six students stopped were closed afterwards.

## 4. Traps

**Notes in this repo expire in days.** Two standing blockers were false when read today: the direction
convention had already been unanimous, and `Figure_2.pdf` had been rendered the day before. Verify
against the primary artifact when it is one command away.

**A grep that finds nothing proves the query did not match.** The double dissociation was reported as
absent from the paper; it was in a caption, phrased differently.

**`\%` is a literal percent sign, not a comment.** A comment-stripping regex that ignores this
destroys text and unbalances braces. It produced a caption measured at 4239 words that is 374.

**Do not use `resumeFromRunId` to re-run an audit after editing the manuscript.** The prompts are
unchanged so the cache replays the old results, and the agents read the files at run time.

**The figures stay on disk.** Dropping a supplement means removing its `\figsupp` line, never the
file: it may be used later.
