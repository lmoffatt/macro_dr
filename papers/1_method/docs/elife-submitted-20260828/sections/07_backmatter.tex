\section*{Data and code availability}
All code and all data behind this study are openly available. The engine, the simulation
configurations that define every run, and the analysis notebooks that draw every figure are
deposited together as \texttt{macro\_ir\_reliability} \citep{moffatt2026code}. The simulation
output the figures are drawn from is deposited separately \citep{moffatt2026data}; it is large,
and the two records are cross-referenced by DOI.

No experimental data were used: every dataset in the paper is simulated. The deposited
configurations regenerate the design, but most of the campaign is not reproducible to the same
numbers, because those lanes drew their random seed from the system entropy source and the drawn
value was not recorded (Materials and methods). For those runs the deposited output is the only
copy, which is why it is deposited rather than left to be regenerated. Two lanes do carry an
explicit seed and do reproduce: the time-resolved dumps behind Figure~\ref{fig:time} and the
differenced-Fisher battery of Supplementary File~1. Every output file stamps the engine commit
that wrote it in its first row, so provenance travels with the file rather than with the
directory holding it.

A separate library, \texttt{macroir} \citep{moffatt2026macroirlib}, carries the
boundary-conditioned likelihood, its score, its Gaussian Fisher information and the distortion
diagnostic of this paper out of the engine and into a small self-contained C++ core, with an R
package and a Python package over it; the diagnostic returns the distortion matrix, its
eigenvalue spectrum and the first-order bias from one call in either language. Its scope is
narrower than the paper's: it carries the boundary-conditioned member and the interval-averaged
least-squares arm, the exact simulator and a Gauss-Newton fit, and none of the other six members,
so the comparisons reported here are reproducible from the engine deposit and not from the
library. Its boundary-conditioned member was checked against the engine on eight cells, four
kinetic schemes, three acquisition intervals spanning a factor of a hundred, a protocol whose
concentration jumps split sampling intervals and a recording with a gap, agreeing on the total
log-likelihood to between $10^{-16}$ and $6\times10^{-15}$ relative and on the score to between
$8\times10^{-14}$ and $1.4\times10^{-9}$. The two language
packages are bindings over that one core, and they were checked against it on a single reference
cell, which is the evidence for the statement that the three interfaces return the same numbers.
