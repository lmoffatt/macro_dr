% TITLE. Reviewed 2026-07-28 against the merged frame, in which the comparison anchor is least
% squares, the contribution is the validity criterion plus the map, and the conclusion is an
% ordering on the design plane rather than a threshold. Three candidates, live one first.
%
% (1) LIVE, below: names the biological system, states the two deliverables, promotes no
%     algorithm, carries no threshold and no "only".
% (2) The title this file carried until now: "Validity limits of time-averaged likelihoods for
%     macroscopic kinetic inference". It reads the failure as a consequence of time averaging,
%     which the results contradict (least squares averages nothing over the window and is the
%     worst-calibrated arm; the boundary-conditioned member averages and is calibrated), and
%     "limits" invites a threshold reading the map does not support. Restorable in one line.
% (3) "Honest uncertainty in kinetic inference from macroscopic ion-channel currents: a
%     validity criterion and a design map". Longer, and "honest" in a title may read as a
%     claim about other people's work rather than about a measured quantity.
% (4) SETTLED 2026-08-04, and this closes the standing title conflict. The previous title,
%     "A validity criterion and a usage map for likelihoods of macroscopic ion-channel currents",
%     promised recommendations that the paper deliberately declines to make: the Discussion now
%     carries an explicit demarcation of what is not settled, and "usage map" contradicts it.
%     The word "map" itself is fine and is the right epistemic word, continuous and cartographic;
%     "usage" is what promises a verdict.
%     The adopted form is the declarative finding recommended by the title study
%     (archive/section-plans-20260729/title.md, revised recommendation), because that is eLife's
%     dominant shape (over half of 20 surveyed titles, zero colons, zero questions, zero aphorisms)
%     and it is the grammar of Luciano's most-cited paper. Reordered to the grammar of
%     moffatt2007estimation, "Estimation of Ion Channel Kinetics from Fluctuations of Macroscopic
%     Currents": "ion channel" open and attached to the kinetic information, "macroscopic currents"
%     unmodified. That echo is free and it removes the hyphen question.
%     Rejected in the same pass: "Measuring/Quantifying how ..." (gerund is eLife's tertiary shape
%     and reads as a procedure rather than a finding; the novelty scoping it bought is already done,
%     and better, by the narrow-novelty paragraph in the Introduction), and any form carrying
%     "differ" or "differently" (the plural already says a family is compared, and it shifts the
%     emphasis away from the fact that the best member distorts too, which the Discussion defends).
\title{Likelihood approximations distort the ion channel kinetic information in macroscopic currents}
