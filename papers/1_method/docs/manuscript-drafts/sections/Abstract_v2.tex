\begin{abstract}
A mechanism can leave a mark no model is needed to see: the flip state of P2X$_2$ showed up as a delay before the current rises. The asymmetric activation reported from the same recordings two decades later has no such signature: it rests on ratios of evidence between schemes, and changing the likelihood approximation changed which mechanism came first. Where such approximations break was never located, for want of a known truth: this gating process simulates exactly where its likelihood cannot be evaluated exactly. The instruments are then classical: at the true parameters the score, the log-likelihood's gradient, averages to zero, and its covariance across recordings equals the Fisher information reported. We scored eight members, from least squares to a filter conditioning each interval average on both ends, over channel number, noise and interval, ten thousand recordings per cell. Members that leave the correlation unmodelled misreport their information by an order of magnitude wherever the residual retains memory, and no single factor repairs it, because the distortion has directions. One member, conditioning on both ends, is calibrated over almost the whole plane, and its own departures are located too. No region of the measured plane gives both information about the unitary current and a trustworthy least-squares interval. The member, its score and Fisher information and the exact simulator are available for R and Python, so the measurement can be run on another scheme.
\end{abstract}
